# Prize eligibility decision

**Stay an INDIVIDUAL entrant.** Recommendation: do not change.

- **Grand Prize ($50k)** is awarded to the highest final score across all categories and is open
  to any eligible submission — an individual entry is fully eligible. Nothing is gained by
  switching.
- **Taskmaster ($20k)** — entered.
- **Individual/Hobbyist (2 × $10k)** — an individual entry is eligible; an organization entry
  would NOT be.
- **Startup Excellence ($20k)** requires an incorporated organization and a corporate email.
  Switching would forfeit Individual/Hobbyist eligibility and change IP-ownership and
  prize-recipient representations days before the deadline, for no increase in the ceiling
  (a submission can win a maximum of one prize).

Cleanest truthful configuration: individual entrant, Taskmaster track, Grand Prize considered
automatically.
