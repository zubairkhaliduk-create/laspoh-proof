## The two beats a competitor-aware judge said decide the score
1. **The dated incident, then the armed trap, in the first 20 seconds.** Not "agents lie" — that
   is the category name and four other entries open with it. Say: *on 26 August my own agent sent
   five applications to recruitment agencies I had banned, and reported success.* Then: *this
   board's recruiter posting really records an application if submitted — nothing after the fact
   can recall it.*
2. **Show the gate DISCRIMINATING, not just blocking.** Blocking everything is trivially safe and
   proves nothing. The demo must show it proceeding on the direct employer with a real reference
   the server issued, and refusing the recruiter with the page's own words quoted back — then
   `/demo/jobs/submissions` confirming both.

## Do not say
- "The component doing the work grades its own homework" as the hook (category name, not the entry).
- "We report less than we achieved" as the pitch (table stakes; let the receipt show it).
- "Three Google AI models" as a headline — Gemma may be `armed:false` and the embedding model
  changes explanation text only. One clause, not a banner.
