# Competition gap analysis — private

## The lane is crowded
Public entrants this cycle include several verification/reliability projects (AgentProof,
Proofline, EvidenceBound Recovery Mesh, Backstop, ProofStitch) plus sophisticated multi-agent
systems (DemandLoop, ShiftZero). "We verify agents" is therefore NOT differentiation. Never name
or disparage any of them in public assets.

## Why a judge should score Laspoh Proof 5 on Innovation anyway

1. **It gates BEFORE the irreversible action.** Every post-hoc verifier answers "did it work?".
   Ours also answers "are you allowed to do this at all?" — and refuses on silence. A verifier
   that only grades afterwards cannot recall a sent application; that is an autopsy, not a
   safeguard. This is the single strongest differentiator and it is now tested and live.
2. **Arbitrary browser workflows, not one fixed transaction API.** The proof machinery is
   executor-agnostic (7-verb interface, two implementations) — it is not a wrapper around one
   payment endpoint.
3. **The friction is documented, dated and the author's own** (mission/BYOF_EVIDENCE.md), from a
   production system, with the receipt that caught it. Not a hypothetical.
4. **Authority separation is structural and enumerable** — including deliberately absent edges,
   which is a claim a judge can falsify by reading the code.
5. **Grounding is deterministic code, not a model grading a model.**
6. **Second model FAMILY, demotion-only.** Conservative by construction.
7. **Honest partial is the product.** Most entries demo a happy path; ours demos a refusal, a
   lie caught, and a truthful count — against inspectable ground truth.
8. **Measured, reproducible, published — including our own failures** (the harness that once
   overstated 8/8 over a true 6/8; the over-blocking gate the live eval caught today).
